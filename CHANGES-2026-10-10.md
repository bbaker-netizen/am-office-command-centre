# Payroll page - 2026-10-10

Built on v53 (email sign-in, People and access). New Payroll page in Office, beside Money, for accounts that see Money. View-only accounts can see the report and the entry but cannot post or change settings.

What it does
- Reads one pay period of JobTread timesheets and splits every employee's hours into the cost of sales buckets Danielle posts to: Demolition, Asbestos, Lead, Mould, Sampling, Restoration and Other, each Edmonton or Calgary. Shop and office time stays in general payroll.
- Service line comes from the cost code on the time entry. City comes from the job's Estimating branch, or the address if the branch is blank.
- Jobs 26-00000 General Shop and 26-0000 Office Work always stay in general payroll. More can be added in Payroll settings.
- ACES temp time types are left out of payroll and shown separately. Anyone still clocked in is left out until they clock out.
- Download summary and Download detail give Danielle the report as CSV files.
- Journal entry: type each person's payroll cost, press Show the entry, then Post to QuickBooks. Overtime counts at 1.5 when the pay is split. One entry per pay period, numbered PAYSPLIT-YYMMDD and dated the last day of the period. The app refuses a second post for the same period.
- Payroll settings: the QuickBooks account, class and location for each bucket, the payroll expense account the money comes out of, the first day of a pay period (default 2026-09-28, two weeks) and the overtime multiplier.

Files
- public/command.html: Payroll tab, page and settings. Footer and the Money area description on People and access updated.
- netlify/functions/payroll.mjs: new. /api/payroll. Uses the v53 account sign-in.
- netlify/functions/_payroll.mjs: new. The rules, as plain functions.
- netlify/functions/_qbo.mjs: adds qboPost, used only by the payroll journal entry.

Not done, on purpose
- Hours are not sent into QuickBooks as time activities. QuickBooks needs each hour's pay type (regular or overtime), and that list is only open to apps with Intuit's payroll permission. Without it overtime would arrive as regular time.
