# Changes 2026-10-20: Payroll is its own box

## What it does

Payroll used to come with Money. Anyone who could see payroll could also see the company's money and buying pages, and the other way round.

Now Payroll is its own box on People and access, beside Office, Money, Field and Assistants. An admin can give someone payroll without Money, or Money without payroll.

## Nobody loses anything on the day it goes live

Everyone who had Money before this change is given Payroll too, once, so they see exactly what they saw yesterday. After that, an admin can untick Payroll for a Money person and it stays unticked.

The shortcuts on People and access now include Payroll where they always did in practice:

- Whole company: Office, Money, Payroll, Field, Assistants.
- Office: Office, Money, Payroll, Assistants.
- Estimating and Field: no Payroll, same as before.

Payroll on its own is not enough to save an account. Tick at least one of Office, Money or Field as well, the same rule the Assistants box already follows.

## Example

Jen sees Office and Assistants. To give her payroll without the money pages, tick Payroll on her account. Her responsibility then reads Custom. She refreshes the page and the Payroll tab is there.
