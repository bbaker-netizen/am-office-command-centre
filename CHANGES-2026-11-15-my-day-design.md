# Changes 2026-11-15: My day redesigned

Phone version am-inv-v85.

Bruce: My day was squashed, hard to read and not engaging. Redesigned. Nothing it does has changed; only how it reads.

## What changed

- **A clear top.** Today's date, the greeting, and one plain sentence: how many things are on the list, how many are past due, how many emails need you, how many chases are ready for a call.
- **Number tiles** under it: on your list, past due (red when above zero), emails that need you, and for Craig room on the line and the end of this week (press either to open Cash flow).
- **Bigger talk bar**: a large Talk button, a large box and Add, the same height, easy to hit on a phone.
- **The check-in reads like a note**, with its numbered list shown as a real list, and a cyan edge so it stands out.
- **The hit list breathes**: larger titles, and coloured tags for what it is (pay, call, coming in), the amount, and the due date as a day ("Due Sat, Nov 21"; red when past). Move to top and Remove sit to the side.
- **Money in its own column** on a computer: Cash flow (two big numbers and up to three lines of advice), Bank since you last looked (money in and out, name on the left, amount on the right, totals in the heading), and Due this week (to invoice, ready for Thursday billing first, and to pay with the total).
- **Done, not billed** shows the closest to billing first, eight at a time, with filter buttons by reason and their counts (Ready for Thursday billing, Waiting on the PM, Loose ends on site, and so on). Missing paperwork shows as red tags.
- **Headings** are plain words in normal case, not small grey capitals.
- **On a phone** everything stacks in the order it matters: talk, check-in, list, cash flow, inbox, bank, due this week, jobs. The tiles sit two by two.
