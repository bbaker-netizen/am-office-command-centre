# A&M Office Command Centre - v45 (2026-10-08)

v45 changes the name only. The app now calls itself A&M Office Command Centre everywhere a person sees it: the browser tab, the phone's home screen (A&M Command, because longer names get cut off) and the command centre page. Nothing else changed.

## Rename the Netlify site at the same time
1. In Netlify, open the site called am-inventory.
2. Site configuration, then General, then Site details, then Change site name.
3. Type am-office-command-centre and save. Netlify only allows small letters, numbers and hyphens.
4. The address becomes https://am-office-command-centre.netlify.app. The old address stops working.

Do this before the Google setup, because Google is given the app's address. Then tell everyone the new address. Phones that added the app to their home screen need to open the new address and add it again.

# A&M app - v44 (2026-10-06)

Three additions on top of v43: a Buying section, changing jobs from the app, and a place to read and send the robots' emails.

## Buying (owner and office)
- **Order for the shop.** Shelf lines at or below their minimum, grouped by supplier. Each line shows how much to order and what it should cost at the price last paid.
- **Purchase orders out.** Open purchase orders from JobTread, with supplier, job, cost and due date. Overdue ones are flagged.
- **Bills to pay.** Every unpaid supplier bill from QuickBooks by due date: past due, due this week, and all unpaid.
- **Who we spend with.** Spend by supplier since 1 April, from QuickBooks.
- **Supplier and subtrade paperwork.** Every supplier we owe money to, or have an open PO with, checked for WCB clearance and insurance expiry on its JobTread record.
  - Today nobody in JobTread has WCB clearance ticked, and no insurance expiry dates are filled in, so expect a long list at first.

## Changing a job from the app (owner, office, field)
- **The job drawer has a "Change this job" box.** It covers Status, Project Manager, start date, crew size, days of work, risk level, and notice of project filed and expiry.
- **Written straight to JobTread.** It uses the app's JobTread key, with JobTread's own emails switched off.
- **Values are checked before sending.** Every change is logged with who made it, under Settings, JobTread link, recent activity.
- **Project manager names** come from `PM_NAMES` in Netlify (default `Ryan,Pat,George`).
- **Untested against live JobTread.** These writes have only been tested against a stand-in. Change one thing on the TEST job (99-00001) first and check it in JobTread.

## To send: the robots' emails (owner and office)
- **The flow.** The robots write drafts into one mailbox, automation@amabatement.com. The app lists them. Press one to read it in full, then **Send it now**, **Discard**, or **Change it in Gmail first**.
- **How it is sent.** Gmail does the sending, so it leaves from that mailbox and a copy sits in its Sent folder. Every send and discard is logged with who did it.
- **Only one mailbox.** The connection refuses any mailbox except `MAILBOX`, default automation@amabatement.com.

### One-time Google setup (Workspace admin, about 15 minutes)
1. Go to Google Cloud console, signed in as an A&M admin. Create a project called "A&M app".
2. Turn on the Gmail API.
3. Set up the OAuth consent screen: user type **Internal**, app name "A&M app", scope `gmail.compose`.
4. Under Credentials, create an OAuth client of type Web application.
   - Authorised redirect URI: `https://<your site>/api/mail?action=callback`.
5. In Netlify, set `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI` (the same address as step 4). Redeploy.
6. In the app, as Jen or Craig, open To send, press **Connect automation@amabatement.com**, and sign in to Google **as automation@**.

### For the robots to draft into automation@
The 16 morning robots run in Craig's Claude account today, so their drafts land in **Craig's** Gmail.
- **Move them.** Sign in to Claude as the Automation account, connect Gmail (automation@), JobTread, QuickBooks and Google Drive, and recreate the 16 scheduled tasks.
  - The prompts are in the Automation Account Migration Pack in the project.
  - Then pause the copies in Craig's account.
- **Replies.** Set automation@ to forward replies to estimating@, so answers from clients reach a person.

# One A&M app - v43 (2026-10-06)

v43 makes this the single A&M application. The command centre now lives inside it at `/command.html`, and the finance console's useful parts are in its Money tab. When this is live, the separate finance console site can be switched off.

## What v43 adds

- **Command centre inside the app.** The button sits next to Settings on the shop and office screens. It shows for the owner, the office and the field leads, and is hidden from crew. It uses the same sign-in, so nobody signs in twice.
  - Owner: every tab.
  - Office: Overview, Estimating, Dispatch, Money, Records.
  - Field: Overview, Projects, Sites, Closeout, Equipment.
- **One data door, `/api/command`.** It reads JobTread with the app's JobTread key and QuickBooks through the app's QuickBooks connection, all on the server. Nobody needs their own JobTread or QuickBooks login to see their section. Who sees what is decided on the server:
  - Crew get nothing.
  - Field get no money.
  - Answers are kept for one to five minutes, so ten people opening it at 7 am cost one read.
- **Money, built from the books.** QuickBooks reports are read so each customer and supplier is counted once. The page shows:
  - Cash position.
  - Who owes us and who we owe.
  - Whether the books are safe to price from.
  - Break even.
  - What an hour of labour really costs.
  - A 13-week bank balance draft.
  - Sales pace.
- **Equipment, from the shop's own records.** This shows machines by location, shelf lines at or below minimum, and gear and material by job matched to JobTread. Closed jobs that still have gear are flagged, and so are missing machines.
- **Safer QuickBooks refresh.** The QuickBooks connection is shared by every part of the app and refreshes under a lock, so two refreshes at once cannot kill each other's token.
- **The phone learns its role.** It finds out on every sync, so changing someone's role in Netlify reaches their phone within a minute.

## Set these in Netlify (in addition to the v42 list)

| Name | Example | Sees |
|---|---|---|
| `OWNER_USERS` | `Craig Williams` | Everything |
| `OFFICE_USERS` | `Jen Garrison` | Office tabs and money (also the shop office screens) |
| `FIELD_USERS` | `Amir Faiz,Ryan,Pat,George` | Field tabs, no money |

Names must match the app's people list exactly. Anyone not listed is crew. Until these are set, the command centre is closed to everyone.

QuickBooks needs `QBO_CLIENT_ID`, `QBO_CLIENT_SECRET`, `QBO_REDIRECT_URI` (and `QBO_ENVIRONMENT` if sandbox). If the inventory app already had these, nothing changes. Reconnect QuickBooks once from Settings with the new Connect button.

JobTread uses the key saved in Settings, JobTread link. Use a key issued to A&M (the automation account), not a person.

## Switching off the separate finance console

1. Deploy v43 and set the roles above.
2. Open the command centre as Craig and check the Money tab against QuickBooks. Receivables should match the balance sheet.
3. In the old console, use Settings, Backup to download its data. File that backup in the Finance shared drive in Google Drive.
4. In Netlify, delete the `am-financial-console` site. That also takes down its public `seed.json` and its stored staff pay list.
5. In the Intuit developer portal, remove the console's redirect address, or revoke its app, so its QuickBooks connection stops working.

Left behind on purpose:
- **The staff pay list.** QuickBooks Payroll owns that.
- **The hand-typed cash lines.** The 13-week draft replaces them.

If Craig wants a line of credit limit or a budget figure on the Money tab, that is a small add: an office-only settings box.

  - Paging through 60 jobs.
  - Each role's access.
  - The receivables parent-customer case.
  - Balance sheet and profit and loss read by group.
  - Token refresh.
  - The short cache.
- A headless walk-through signing in as owner, office, field and crew, then opening the command centre:

# Inventory app changes - 2026-10-06 (v42)

For Bruce. Deploy this over v41. Nothing in the data needs converting.

## Before you deploy: set these in Netlify

Site configuration, then Environment variables. Then redeploy.

| Name | What to put | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `AUTH_SECRET` | A long random string (40+ characters). Generate one, never reuse. | The key that signs everyone in. Without it the key sits in the data store. Setting it signs everyone out once. |
| `OFFICE_USERS` | Names exactly as they appear in the app, comma separated. For example `Jen Garrison,Craig Williams,Max` | Only these people can change people, PINs, settings, the JobTread and QuickBooks connections, or wipe history. If it is empty, everyone is treated as office, as before. |
| `FEED_TOKEN` | Another long random string (at least 24 characters). | Turns on the read-only summary at `/api/feed`. Leave it unset to keep the feed off. |
| `TOKEN_DAYS` | Optional. Default 30. | How long a phone stays signed in before it needs the PIN again with signal. |
| `JT_PUSH_ENABLED` | Leave unset. | Sending cost to JobTread stays off until it is rebuilt and tested on a practice job. |

## After you deploy

1. Make sure at least one person has a PIN. The sign-in gate now stays on for good once it has been on.
2. Open the office settings and reconnect QuickBooks with the new Connect button (the old link no longer works).
3. Have Jen add a test person, issue a PIN, and sign in on a phone that has never had the app. Their name should be on the sign-in list straight away.

## What changed

### Jen's fault: new members cannot sign in, "data not found"
- The sign-in screen now gets the list of names from the office (`GET /api/auth`, names and has-PIN only), not from the phone's own old copy.
- A phone that is refused no longer treats that as "the office has nothing" and no longer tries to make the starter file the company's list.
- Whether sign-in is needed is remembered from the office, so an old phone cannot decide nobody has a PIN.
- Straight after signing in, the phone syncs and replaces the starter list with the real one instead of merging the two.
- Saves that are not allowed or not possible now say why in plain words instead of failing silently or showing "auth".
- Removing a person now sticks on every phone.

### Security
- The sign-in gate cannot switch itself off again once anybody has had a PIN.
- Office-only actions are checked on the server (see `OFFICE_USERS`). A crew phone can no longer set anyone's PIN, switch people off, change settings, change the JobTread key, link or bill in JobTread, connect or disconnect QuickBooks, or wipe history.
- QuickBooks connect needs an office sign-in. The one-time code expires after ten minutes and works once.
- Sign-in tokens expire (30 days by default) and are no longer accepted in the web address.
- Wrong PINs are counted so a burst of guesses cannot slip past, plus a second limit per connection (30 wrong in an hour).
- Any old JobTread key left in the catalog settings is stripped before the catalog goes to phones.
- The server records who was signed in when each movement arrived (`sentBy`), alongside the name the phone wrote.

### Data safety
- Movements from two phones arriving at the same moment can no longer overwrite each other. Pushes now take a short lock.
- Two catalog saves at the same moment can no longer both win. One is told to retry.
- Needs `@netlify/blobs` 10 (package.json updated) for the conditional writes.
- A save that has to be retried no longer downloads the whole movement history.
- The sync position is compared as a number, so phones stop re-pulling the same movements.

### Other fixes
- Five "today" checks used the UTC date, so they went wrong after about 6 pm in Edmonton. Fixed.
- Matching an inventory job to JobTread can now search every JobTread job by number or name, not just the newest 25.
- The JobTread link check now fails safe: if it cannot check, it does not write.
- QuickBooks vendor list raised from 200 to 1,000.
- New: `GET /api/feed` read-only summary for the command centre (shelf stock, low stock, machines and material out per job with the JobTread id, gear on closed jobs, missing machines). Off until `FEED_TOKEN` is set.

## Still open
- Sending material cost to JobTread (`pushCost`) needs rebuilding so the lines are worked out on the server from the movement log, plus a map from shelf items to JobTread cost codes. Then a test on a practice job.
- Kits are still empty and still block Close job if a kit tag is scanned out.
- PINs are still 4 digits hashed once on the phone. The counters make guessing slow; a longer PIN would be better.

## How it was tested
Run locally against an in-memory stand-in for Netlify Blobs: 38 server checks (sign-in, roles, gate, QuickBooks connect, token expiry, 60 movements pushed by 12 phones at once with none lost, two saves at once, feed) and a headless phone walk-through (new person added by the office, signs in on a fresh phone, sees the real jobs). Not yet run on Netlify itself.
